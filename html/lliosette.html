About LLiosette

LLiosette is the creator of Binary (more below), GrassCats, Multicategore, and Multi-Cata. He has collaborated with certain artists such as Mozeke and Amanda. His notable releases are GrassCats and the Binary series (Soundtrack 1 and 2).

What is Binary?

Binary is a youtube series created by LLiosette. While Binary is technically mostly a music project, every episode includes static scenes of rooms, dreams, and fears. These scenes have a line-boil aesthetic with a low effort vibe. That sounds worse than it is. It goes over mental health topics similar to OMORI, if you even know what that is.
